The page notices when you stop

idle state · one small event
idle 0.0sstate awake lamp 100%hint stop moving for eight seconds

Idleness is the one input every interface ignores. After eight seconds of stillness the lamp dims, dust catches what is left of the light, and a moth arrives — then it loops, slowly, and the scene has a life of its own.

Any pointer movement resets it instantly and the lamp comes back. The rule is that idle behaviour must never cost the user anything: it is decoration on a timer, not a task.
